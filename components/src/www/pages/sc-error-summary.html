Error summary

Lists every error at the top of the page after a submission. Focus moves to it; it is a group named by its heading, with no role="alert" (CMP-05).

Example

Enter the plate code Enter the plate number using numbers only, like 12345

Follow a link to move focus to its field. On a real page focus moves to the summary on load; this example uses no-autofocus.

Notes

Error-container fill, a 2px error border, 8px corners. Links in error, Bold and underlined, each at least 44 × 44. The Arabic heading is "توجد مشكلة" (W1). Dark: error-20 fill, error-80 border and links.
