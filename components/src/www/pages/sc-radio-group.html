This card is always lang="ar" dir="rtl". The radios sit on the right and the error bar is on the right (start) side.

How the accessibility works

The group renders a fieldset with role="radiogroup" and one native radio per sc-radio, all in its own shadow root. Each sc-radio is slotted into its radio's label with manual slot assignment, so its content names the radio while the radios stay in one tree.
