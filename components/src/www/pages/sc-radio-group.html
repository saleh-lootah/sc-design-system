This card is always lang="ar" dir="rtl". The radios sit on the right and the error bar is on the right (start) side.

How the accessibility works

The group renders a native fieldset with role="radiogroup" in the page's DOM (light DOM, decision A2), and each sc-radio renders a native radio and its label. All the radios share one name in one tree, so the browser provides the radio group behaviour: one Tab stop, and the arrow keys move and select.
