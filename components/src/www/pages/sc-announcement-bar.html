Announcement bar

A site-wide announcement above the header: planned maintenance or a service outage. A labelled region, not an alert.

Tones

Planned maintenance on Saturday 25 October. Payment services will be unavailable from 2am to 6am (Dubai time).
You can now renew your driving licence in the app.
Payment services are not working at the moment. We are fixing it.

Dismissing hides the bar and moves focus to the next element. With announcement-id the dismissal is remembered in this browser.
