# Review 2: contradictions in Shared Channels UX Standard 2.0

Reviewer: contradiction critic. Scope: `docs/shared-channels-ux-standard.md` (all 2,157 lines), checked against `docs/v2/decisions.md`, `docs/v2/foundations.md`, `docs/v2/component-language.md`, `components/specs/*.md`, `components/COMPONENTS.md` and, where a number was in doubt, `tokens/*.json` and `components/perf-budgets.json`. Read-only except for this file.

## Summary

**41 contradictions: 2 Blocker, 8 Major, 31 Minor.**

What the review found:

- **Two requirements cannot be met by a service that follows CMP-01** ("use the `sc-` component"). The shipped `sc-footer`, `sc-utility-bar` and `sc-language-switch` render nothing without JavaScript, but PERF-04 says help and navigation MUST work without it. `sc-breadcrumbs` gives its links a 24px target, but A11Y-04 asks for 44px. The service team cannot fix either one. Both are Blockers.
- **Links (CMP-08).** The standard requires `channel-action` underlines "in every state". Its own CHR-06 breaks that rule, and so do five built components.
- **The UAE PASS rule (CMP-11, U3a) is right in the standard, but stale text elsewhere still says city indigo for sign-in.** The stale wording is in `foundations.md`, decision Q-header and the `sc-button` spec, and teams read all three.
- **PLAT-05 tells native teams to build from `COMPONENTS.md`.** Most of that file is still v1 and contradicts at least eight v2 rules.
- **The standard's own Arabic example breaks its gender rule.** ELIG uses «قد تحتاج», which CNT-11 bans. The utility bar's built string «اتصل» breaks it too.
- **The standard has not caught up with some decisions and specs.** The fieldset legend role (W11), the docked-bar shadow (U4 against W23), dialog surfaces, the stepper halo and the `radius-full` list all disagree.
- **Some decision entries contradict each other or the tokens:** D11, Q-header, W21 and the role-table "tags" line. `decisions.md` says it wins, so these errors spread unless someone fixes them.

What was checked by script:

- Every requirement identifier (347) is defined once. No identifier is referenced without a definition, and every OQ reference resolves. Two links point to the wrong anchor (row 36).
- Appendix A has 55 criteria (31 A and 24 AA), which matches A11Y-01.
- The type scale's leading ratios match FND-13.
- The budget table in PERF-08 matches `perf-budgets.json`.
- Motion durations match `tokens/motion.json`: 120, 150, 200, 160, 280 and 600ms.

Severity:

- **Blocker:** a service cannot satisfy both requirements, or the standard asks for something an assessment must fail.
- **Major:** two binding sources disagree on behaviour or appearance that teams will build or that panels will assess.
- **Minor:** wording, naming, numbers or stale documents that mislead but do not force a failure.

"Which should win" follows the order the documents declare. `decisions.md` beats `foundations.md` and `component-language.md`. Token files beat the numbers in prose. The standard beats specs on requirements, unless a later decision changed the requirement.
