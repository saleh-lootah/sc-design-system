## 1. Summary verdict

**Broadly aligned, with a small number of real failure risks.** The standard is stronger than most government standards on the things WCAG 2.2 added. It sets 44px targets (above 2.5.8, close to AAA 2.5.5). It has a single high-contrast focus ring that is measured in the token build. It reserves space for docked bars so focus is not obscured (2.4.11). It has a timeout rule that quotes 2.2.1 correctly and closes the "security" loophole. It handles redundant entry across entity boundaries (3.3.7). Arabic language, direction and bidirectional text get unusually careful treatment.

Appendix A is complete and correct in scope: it lists all 55 Level A and AA criteria (31 A and 24 AA, counted), and it treats 4.1.1 Parsing as obsolete, which is what WCAG 2.2 says.

The weaknesses fall into three groups:

1. **Component details that would fail in testing.** The session-timeout dialog's Escape and drag behaviour is undefined, and the dialog has no description for assistive technology. Sticky regions *inside* components (the `sc-table` scroll column, and the weekday row and footer of the `sc-date-range` sheet) can cover focus. The date-range sheet may not fit 320 CSS px. MOT-09's "respond on press" wording invites pointer-down activation, which fails 2.5.2. Bidi isolate characters can travel into pasted codes and fail validation.
2. **Criteria with no body requirement.** Appendix A lists every criterion, but several have nothing behind them in the chapters: media (1.2.x), sensory characteristics in a mirrored layout (1.3.3), tooltips (1.4.13), character key shortcuts (2.1.4, except quick exit), label in name (2.5.3) and CAPTCHA under 3.3.8. Documents (PDF receipts) and third-party widgets (chat, Happiness Meter, "accessibility options") are not covered either.
3. **Consistency between the standard and its design decisions.** The "Bold for current" cue disappears in Arabic, where short interface text is already Bold (D12). Consistent help (3.2.6) is not reconciled across channel pages and transaction pages. Some components (the date-range month buttons, breadcrumbs) sit below the standard's own 44px rule. CMP-05 describes GOV.UK practice incorrectly.

None of these needs a change of direction. With the fixes below, the standard could credibly claim WCAG 2.2 AA and also adopt five AAA criteria it already almost meets: 2.4.12, 2.4.13, 2.5.5, 2.2.5 and 2.3.3.

**Counts:** 7 fail risks, 12 gaps and 11 improvements, 30 findings in all. In the coverage matrix, 35 of the 55 criteria are adequately covered, 14 partly and 6 not at all (beyond the register row).

## 4. Beyond AA: what a Dubai government service should adopt

### 4.1 AAA criteria worth adopting, most of them already met

| Criterion | Status in this standard | Recommendation |

### 4.2 EN 301 549 and Section 508 equivalence

- **EN 301 549 V4.1.1** was published by ETSI on 2 September 2026 and moves the web baseline to WCAG 2.2 A and AA. Until it is cited in the EU Official Journal, V3.2.1 (WCAG 2.1) remains the presumption-of-conformity baseline. A service that meets this standard meets the web clause 9 of either version (V3.2.1 also has 4.1.1: F-23). The clauses that are **not** WCAG, and that matter here:
  - **5.3 Biometrics:** do not rely on a single biometric characteristic. Relevant to UAE PASS face verification; the assisted route covers it.
  - **6 Two-way voice communication:** call centre routes (the utility bar's "Call 04") need an alternative for deaf and speech-impaired users: real-time text, video relay or a text channel. HELP-02 partly covers this.
  - **7 Video capabilities:** players must show captions and audio description controls at the same level as play controls (F-12).
  - **10 Non-web documents** (F-15). **11 Software** applies to the native apps, including 11.7 user preferences and 11.8 authoring tools (staff tools that produce content).
  - **12 Documentation and support services:** support must be able to communicate in accessible formats and describe the accessibility features. Add this to 13.17 and to Appendix C.
- **Section 508** (US) refers to WCAG 2.0 AA, which this standard exceeds, plus 4.1.1 and the functional performance criteria (chapter 3). It is only relevant if a supplier contract cites it; F-23 covers the 4.1.1 difference.
- **ISO/IEC 40500:2025** is WCAG 2.2. Cite it where UAE or Dubai procurement prefers ISO standards (F-25). Whether a UAE instrument mandates a level remains OQ-6.

### 4.3 Arabic assistive technology realities to design and test for

- **Automatic language switching depends on installed voices.** `lang` is necessary but not sufficient. The test record should name the voices (finding F-28), and line 411's instruction should say "an Arabic voice and an English voice installed".
- **Latin digits in Arabic are read as Arabic number words** by all major Arabic voices, which is what users expect. Isolated Latin letters (plate codes, "F-" prefixes) are spelled out with Arabic letter names by some voices. Test the confirmation page (line 361) with NVDA plus eSpeak NG and plus OneCore, and with TalkBack.
- **Caret movement in mixed-direction fields** is unreliable in some browsers. `sc-text-input ltr` (LANG-17) is the right mitigation. Keep the LTR-field e2e tests in Arabic.
- **Speech input in Arabic is limited** on the main platforms (F-20), and **Arabic braille** is supported but rarely tested (F-28).
- **Mirrored focus order:** VoiceOver and TalkBack swipe order follows the DOM. LANG-14 (DOM order equals reading order, no `row-reverse`) is exactly right and should stay a MUST.

## 5. The design system decisions

| Decision | Assessment | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **U1 neutral focus ring** (neutral-10 light, white dark, 3px, 2px offset, "on" colour inside fills) | **Sound; better than a channel-coloured ring.** It removes the risk of a pale channel failing 3:1, and every measured pair is at least 6.46:1 (FOC-03), most above 12:1. `outline` survives forced colours, and `Highlight` is used there. It meets AA 2.4.7 and 1.4.11, and AAA 2.4.13. | Watch nested contexts: a filled band inside a dark `data-theme` footer inside a light page must rebind `--sc-color-focus-ring` correctly. | **W19 inset ring at edges** | **Sound.** A ring that stays whole beats one clipped by the viewport, and 2.4.7 and 2.4.13 both judge the visible indicator. ## 6. Recommended additions

In priority order. Each is short enough to drop into the standard.

1. **TIME-02 addition:** "Pressing Escape, or any way of closing the warning other than 'Sign out', extends the session. The warning appears at least 2 minutes before expiry, is described to assistive technology by its message and time left, and cannot be dismissed by dragging or by the scrim. Activity in any tab extends the session in all of them."
2. **A11Y-05 addition:** "Sticky or fixed regions inside components (table columns, sheet headers and footers) reserve their size in their scroll container with `scroll-padding`. Page bars become static when the viewport is too short for them (TPL-01, CHR-05). Focus MUST NOT be hidden, even partly, by anything the library draws (WCAG 2.4.12)."
3. **New ASK-14 (bot protection):** the text in F-7.
4. **LANG-21 and ASK-03 amendment:** strip bidirectional formatting and zero-width characters before validation (F-16).
5. **MOT-09 reword:** feedback on press, action on release (F-5).
6. **A11Y-06 addition:** a current or selected state never relies on weight alone, because Arabic short text is already Bold (F-8). Fix the footer's Channels group.
7. **HELP-01 rewrite:** define the set of pages, and list the help mechanisms and their order for both shells (F-10).
8. **New 9.7 Audio and video** (F-12) and **new 14.5 or 11.10 Documents** (F-15).
9. **New CMP rules:** content on hover or focus (F-11); label in name (F-20); no single-key shortcuts (F-21); third-party widgets and no overlays (F-14); shadow-boundary ID references (F-30).
10. **Content rule:** no positional or sensory instructions, especially in mirrored layouts (F-13).
11. **Appendix A upgrades:**
    - Add a "Covered by" column with the requirement IDs from the matrix above.
    - Sharpen the checks for 1.4.10 ("320 CSS px wide; content that scrolls horizontally at 256 CSS px tall; sticky bars at 1280 × 720 at 400%"), 1.3.3 (rewrite as a check: "find instructions that refer to position, shape, colour or sound"), 2.5.8 ("measure hit areas, excluding overlaps"), 3.2.6 ("list each help mechanism and its order on three pages of each shell") and 3.3.8 ("paste a code copied from an Arabic SMS; confirm there is no CAPTCHA").
    - Add a short "AAA adopted" table (section 4.1) and the 4.1.1 procurement note (F-23).
12. **Support matrix:** named Arabic voices, Narrator, an Arabic braille check, speech input and a magnifier (F-28).
13. **Native parity (PLAT-09, PLAT-10):** orientation, platform display settings, and accessibility language on mixed-language text (F-19, F-24).
14. **Bibliography:** close OQ-1 and OQ-2; add ISO/IEC 40500:2025, EN 301 549 V4.1.1 and PDF/UA (F-25).
15. **Component fixes:** 44px targets for the date-range month buttons and breadcrumbs (F-17); a 320px Arabic test for the date-range sheet (F-4); `scroll-padding` in `sc-table` scroll mode and in the date-range sheet (F-2, F-3); inset-ring contrast pairs (F-26).
